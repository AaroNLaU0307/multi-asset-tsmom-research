## 11. 2026-09-28: the T-bill excess-return sensitivity (addendum to section 8)

Done under the delegate decision logged in section 10. Section 8's "Not done" paragraph is
superseded by this one.

**Classification:** ANALYSIS_EXTENSION (QUANT_WORKFLOW_VNEXT §8). The rf = 0 headline (net
Sharpe 0.75, CI [0.29, 1.23]) is unchanged and stays the headline.

**Definition.** The excess return for month t is the core net return for month t minus
`rf_t`, applied once to the whole book:
- The net return is the `net` column of `output/monthly_returns.csv`.
- `rf_t` comes from `data/DGS3MO.csv` under the rule in
  `research/extensions/ca/prospective/ca_rf.py`, whose `lock_rf` is called directly. It takes
  the last non-missing print on or before the decision date, rejects one older than 7
  calendar days, and converts it as Y / 100 / 12.
- The decision date for return month t is the prior calendar month-end, when month t's
  position is set.
- There is no position-level or leverage-scaled financing; that would need a cost model the
  seal does not carry.
- A month whose rate cannot be locked stops the run. None did: the stalest print used is 3
  days old (`rf_decision_date` and `rf_observation_date` in the output).

**Result (sensitivity: net of 3-month T-bill, whole book):** Sharpe 0.61, 95% CI
[0.15, 1.09], over the same 218 months. The CI uses `src/validation.bootstrap_ci` with the
headline's settings (iid percentile, 10,000 resamples, seed 7). Computed by
`run_excess_sharpe.py` (the repository keeps its runners at the root; it has no `scripts/`
directory) into `output/excess_return_sensitivity.csv`. `tests/test_excess_sharpe.py`
recomputes the table from the committed series, and `tests/test_headline.py` recomputes the
two `results/headline.json` stats. The README and `STUDY_SUMMARY.md` report the figure next
to the headline, labelled as a sensitivity.
