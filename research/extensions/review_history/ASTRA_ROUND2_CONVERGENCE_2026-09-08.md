### 3. Residual Issues R1–R6

**R1 — Same-period futures and `confirmed`**

The distinction is valid:

> **Confirmed implementation fact must never imply confirmed strategy-performance edge.**

For example, validating price units, causal roll accounting or reconciliation supports narrowly scoped implementation claims. It creates no independent evidence of future profitability.

However, "the wrapper reproduces the stream within tolerance" is broader than a bookkeeping identity. A non-inferior Sharpe alone does not establish stream reproduction: materially different return paths can have similar Sharpes.

Therefore:

- Define exactly what is being confirmed.
- Verify the permitted `evidence_type` and status under canonical KB/G4 rules.
- Keep empirical wrapper-performance comparisons and mechanical identities distinguishable.
- Do not treat rerunning the same frozen sample as fresh statistical replication.

I accept the conceptual distinction. **The canonical permission and classification remain to be verified.**

**R2 — Mechanical measurements and exposure semantics**

Yes, distinguish at least these three concepts:

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PURE_MECHANICAL_VERIFICATION** | Checks accounting, causality, units or invariants without revealing strategy-performance outcomes. |
| **DESIGN_INFORMING_MEASUREMENT** | Measures feasibility, availability, signal agreement or structure that may influence later choices. |
| **TARGET_PERFORMANCE_EXPOSURE** | Reveals strategy returns, Sharpe, drawdowns, crisis outcomes or other target-performance information. |

These are explanatory categories, **not newly authorized canonical enum values**. Map them to existing vocabulary or an appropriate descriptive field.

They are also not entirely exclusive: a performance exposure can inform design. Keep activity type, outcome exposure and downstream use separately identifiable.

The practical correction is output-based classification. A sanitized reconciliation residual may be mechanical. Displaying a historical strategy PnL series while reconciling it is performance exposure even without comparing two strategies.

Mechanical and design measurements do not automatically become performance trials. Their influence still belongs in lineage. Wave 1 step 1.1 remains exposed baseline analysis, not pure mechanical work.

**R3 — Crisis-retention scope**

**Use claim/objective-specific applicability, not tier membership.**

Apply a crisis-preservation requirement when a candidate is intended to replace the deployed stream while retaining its defensive role, or explicitly claims to preserve that role. Aaron determines the economic requirement and acceptable tradeoff.

Do not apply a crisis-return gate to:

- Accounting reconciliation.
- Contract-specification correctness.
- NAV feasibility.
- Other implementation facts that make no defensive-performance claim.

The tier-M rationale may often align with this rule, but labels should not substitute for the objective. Tier-I as a blanket category is plainly too broad. The 70% figure remains a proposed requirement, not independently justified by this review.

**R4 — X44 score = 0**

Fable's equivalence claim is incorrect.

Unconditional carry estimates performance across all eligible states. Carry conditional on neutral TSMOM estimates performance on a selected state. An unconditional negative does not logically establish that every conditional effect is negative.

That does **not** supply evidence that neutral-state carry works.

The smallest resolution is:

- Keep score=0 outside the current X44 scope for simplicity.
- Describe it as **deferred and untested under this conditional definition**, not already falsified or permanently prohibited.
- Require a separate future hypothesis and accounting if Aaron ever elects to investigate it.
- Do not add another cell or research commitment now.

The proposed ±0.5 bridge also needs to test the direction-changing rule actually contemplated, not merely demonstrate generic carry/trend agreement.

**R5 — X35/X36 lane split**

Yes. Explicitly distinguish:

- **X35a:** descriptive estimator-stability measurement.
- **X35b:** sealed replacement/non-inferiority study.
- **X36a:** cap-binding diagnostic.

These may be stage labels under the existing IDs; four new registry hypotheses are not inherently necessary.

Any Sharpe, worst-month or other strategy-performance results produced during "a" remain exposed. Selecting a candidate and later sealing "b" does not make the same sample independent. The replacement study must preserve that lineage and use acceptance criteria appropriate to its stated objective.

It cannot establish:
