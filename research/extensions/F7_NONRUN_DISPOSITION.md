## §1 What this disposition is — and what it is not

**It is not a falsification.** No F7 hypothesis was tested against any outcome.

**It is not a finding that F7 is unprofitable.** No return, slope, interval or
statistic of any kind was ever computed for F7.

**It is not "seasonality failed again".** The opposite was adjudicated: see §2.

**What it is.** F7 was a scientifically distinct idea. The fully repaired
admissible object became **materially different** from the idea that earned that
distinctness, and the strongest claim that object could support was **too narrow
for the programme's current edge-discovery slot**. The candidate is therefore
removed from the active edge queue and **preserved as a mechanism-study
candidate**, with its return outcome unexposed and available to a future study
that declares the right question.
