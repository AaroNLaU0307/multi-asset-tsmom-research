# Governance records

Process history for this repository: programme closeouts and similar workflow
records written while the research was run under its governed lifecycle.

These are **process records, not research results**. They summarise and point at
the research artifacts; they do not add to or change any finding.

The research entry point is the root [`README.md`](../../README.md).

Contents:

- [`ROUND1_CLOSEOUT.md`](ROUND1_CLOSEOUT.md) — CTA / systematic-macro Discovery Round 1 closeout (derived summary).

Some governance records stay where they are because sealed contracts, manifests or
code point at their current paths: `PROJECT_STATE.md` and `qros-state.yaml` at the
root, and the Owner decision records and ledgers under `ops/`.
